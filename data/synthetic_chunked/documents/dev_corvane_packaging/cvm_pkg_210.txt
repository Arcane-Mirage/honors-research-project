CORVANE MOTORS
Packaging and Labeling Specification
Document ID: CVM-PKG-210
Revision: D
Effective Date: 2025-11-03
All names, companies and document identifiers in this document are fictional.

1 Scope
1.1 This specification defines packaging and labeling requirements for production parts
delivered to Corvane Motors.

2 Packaging Design
2.1 Packaging design shall follow the dimensional guidelines of Packaging Standard
CVM-PKG-050.

2.2 Expendable (one-way) cardboard containers shall be used for all parts weighing less than 2
kg.

3 Labeling
3.1 Labels shall conform to CVM-LBL-220 and shall have a barcode height of no more than 20
mm.

3.2 Label data content shall follow the requirements of the Corvane Supplier Quality Manual.

4 Precedence
4.1 In the event of a conflict between this specification and CVM-CSR-200, the requirements
of this specification shall take precedence.
