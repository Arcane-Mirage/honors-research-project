CORVANE MOTORS
Customer-Specific Requirements: Interior Trim Components
Document ID: CVM-CSR-500
Revision: A
Effective Date: 2026-07-01
All names, companies and document identifiers in this document are fictional.

1 Scope
1.1 This document applies to suppliers of interior trim components delivered to Corvane
Motors.

1.2 Suppliers shall apply this document in place of CVM-CSR-450 Revision C, which this
document supersedes.

2 Materials
2.1 Interior trim materials shall meet the flammability requirements of CVM-MS-610 and the
odor requirements of CVM-MS-612, and the supplier shall submit test results from a
laboratory accredited according to the Corvane laboratory policy CVM-LAB-005.

2.2 Volatile organic compound emissions shall be tested according to CVM-MS-615 at a test
temperature of 65 °C. Results shall be reported in µg/m³ to Corvane Motors within 5 working
days of test completion.

2.3 Materials that contain recycled content shall be declared in the material data system
before part submission. The declaration shall include the supplier name, material grade, and
recycled content percentage.

3 Appearance
3.1 Color and gloss shall match the master sample approved under CVM-AP-300. Visual
evaluation shall be performed under D65 lighting at 1000 lux.

3.2 Grain and texture shall conform to CVM-AP-310 as shown on drawing DRW-7731.

4 Approval of Changes
4.1 Any change to a material formulation shall require written approval from Corvane Motors
before implementation.

4.2 Changes to a material formulation shall be implemented without approval from Corvane
Motors when they are recorded in the supplier's internal change log according to CVM-QS-110.

5 Shipping
5.1 Finished trim parts shall be packaged according to CVM-PKG-210 and shall be protected
from ultraviolet exposure during transport.

5.2 Parts shall be labeled according to CVM-LBL-220 and CVM-PKG-210.

6 Testing
6.1 Test results from internal supplier laboratories shall not be accepted for flammability
testing under CVM-MS-610.

6.2 Odor testing according to CVM-MS-612 shall be performed at 80 °C.

6.3 All material tests shall be performed at a maximum test temperature of 70 °C.
