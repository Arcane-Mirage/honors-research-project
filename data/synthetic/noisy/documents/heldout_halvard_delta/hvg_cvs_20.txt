Scope
This standard follows the chapter structure of the Halvard Q Agreement and contains the
requireme nts of Brennic, Ostra and Global Motors.
Where this standard and the Halvard Q Agreement co ntradict each other, this standard takes
precedence.

0 General provisions
In addition to the Halvard Q Agreement, the supplier shall comply with IATF 16949 throughout
its global supply chain.
Global: Unless exempted in writing, the supplier shall comply with the Glob al Motors
Supplier Warranty Requirements GLB-SWR-001.
Ostra: The supplier shall co mply with the Ostra purchasing standard OST-STD-3868.
Brennic: The Brennic purchasing conditions BRN-PC-001 apply in addition.
