0 General provisions
The supplier shall comply with ISO 9001 and shall apply the Halvard New Parts Program to
every new part.

1 Request and offer
1.1 Offer prerequisites
The supp lier shall submit a comple te offer that covers all documents listed in the request.

1.2 Records
The supplier shall retain quality records for a minimum of 15 years after the end of series
production.

2 Approval
2.1 Part approval
Part approval shall be requested according to the Part Approval Manual HVG-PA-100 before the
first delivery.

2.2 Initial samples
Initial samples shall be delivered no earlier than 10 wo rking days before the planned start
of production.

2.3 Part labels
Part l abels shall show the part number and the date of manufacture.

3 Change management
3.1 Change notification
The supplier shall notify the customer in writing at least 30 days befo re any change to a
manufacturing process.

3.2 Field observation
The supplier shall observe the field performance of its products and report field failures
to the customer.
