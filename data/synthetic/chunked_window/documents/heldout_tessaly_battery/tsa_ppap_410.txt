TESSALY AUTOMOTIVE
Production Part Approval Procedure
Document ID: TSA-PPAP-410
Revision: F
Effective Date: 2025-09-12
All names, companies and document identifiers in this document are fictional.

1 Purpose
1.1 This procedure describes the production part approval process for Tessaly Automotive
suppliers.

2 Submission
2.1 The production part approval package shall be submitted no earlier than 10 working days
before the planned start of production.

2.2 The package shall contain material certifications according to TSA-MAT-420 and a control
plan prepared according to TSA-QP-020.

2.3 Dimensional results shall be reported for all characteristics shown on drawing
TSA-DRW-2210.

2.4 Run-at-rate verification shall be completed before the production part approval package
is submitted.

3 Precedence
3.1 Where this procedure conflicts with any customer-specific requirements document,
including TSA-CSR-400, this procedure shall govern.
