CORVANE MOTORS
Customer-Specific Requirements for Production Part Suppliers
Document ID: CVM-CSR-100
Revision: C
Effective Date: 2026-02-15
All names, companies and document identifiers in this document are fictional.

1 Scope
1.1 This document defines the customer-specific requirements of Corvane Motors for suppliers
of production parts and supplements the purchase order terms.

2 Quality Management System
2.1 The supplier shall maintain a quality management system that conforms to the Corvane
Supplier Quality Manual CVM-SQM-001.

2.2 The supplier shall notify the Corvane supplier quality engineer of any change to its
quality management system certification status within 10 working days.

3 Product Quality Planning
3.1 Control Plans
The supplier shall prepare control plans in accordance with Specification CVM-QS-110 and
shall include all special characteristics identified on Drawing DRW-045.

3.2 The supplier shall perform a process failure mode and effects analysis for every new
part number before the production trial run, using the worksheet format defined in Procedure
QP-12. Part number 55-1020 and part number 55-1021 are excluded from this requirement during
the 2026 model year.

4 Validation Testing
4.1 The supplier shall perform the validation tests listed in the following table:
| Test | Method | Frequency |
| Salt spray corrosion | CVM-TM-305 | Each production lot |
| Coating adhesion | CVM-TM-311 Rev B | Weekly |

4.2 Production parts shall be validated at a maximum test temperature of 85 °C according to
Test Method CVM-TM-320.

4.3 Thermal validation according to CVM-TM-320 shall be performed at a minimum test
temperature of 105 °C for 500 hours.

5 Change Management
5.1 The supplier shall notify Corvane Motors in writing at least 90 days before implementing
any change to a manufacturing process, as required by the Corvane Supplier Quality Manual
CVM-SQM-001.

5.2 Minor process changes shall be implemented no later than 30 days after written
notification to Corvane Motors and shall be documented according to Procedure QP-12.

6 Records
6.1 The supplier shall retain quality records for a minimum of 15 years after the end of
series production, unless a longer period is specified on the applicable drawing.

6.2 Inspection records described in Specification CVM-QS-110 shall be destroyed no later
than 10 years after the end of series production.

7 Engineering Standards
7.1 Fastener Compliance
Fasteners shall conform to Engineering Standard ES 4.12, and coated fasteners shall
additionally meet CVM-ES-210 Rev. B.

7.2 Replacement fasteners supplied for service shall conform to CVM-ES-210 Revision D.
Section 4.12 of this document does not apply to service fasteners.
