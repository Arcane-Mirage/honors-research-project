TESSALY AUTOMOTIVE
Customer-Specific Requirements: Electronic Control Units
Document ID: TSA-CSR-300
Revision: 2
Effective Date: 2026-05-04
All names, companies and document identifiers in this document are fictional.

TSA 1 Introduction
TSA 1.1 This document supplements the general purchasing terms of Tessaly Automotive for
suppliers of electronic control units.

TSA 2 Design and Development
TSA 2.1 The supplier shall submit the design failure mode and effects analysis to Tessaly
Automotive at each design review, and the analysis shall follow the methodology described in
Tessaly procedure TSA-QP-031; where the analysis identifies a severity rating of 9 or 10,
the supplier shall additionally document the mitigation actions according to TSA-QP-032 and
shall report the results of all associated validation tests using the template in
TSA-QF-031A.

TSA 2.2 Software for electronic control units shall be developed according to TSA-SW-500
rev.3.

TSA 2.3 Software release notes shall reference TSA-SW-500 Rev 4 for all releases issued
after 1 July 2026.

TSA 3 Manufacturing
TSA 3.1 Printed circuit board assemblies shall be cleaned according to the TSA-MP-210
specification, and ionic contamination shall not exceed 1.0 µg/cm² NaCl equivalent.

TSA 3.2 Conformal coating shall be applied in accordance with Specification TSA MP 215 and
cured for at least 30 minutes.

TSA 3.3 Coated assemblies shall be inspected under ultraviolet light as described in
TSA-MP-215, and assemblies with coating voids larger than 0.5 mm shall be rejected.

TSA 3.4 All solder joints shall be inspected by automated optical inspection on 100% of
production units.

TSA 3.5 Solder joint inspection shall be performed on a sampling basis of 5 units per
production lot, as defined in TSA-QP-040.

TSA 4 Traceability
TSA 4.1 Each electronic control unit shall carry a serial number label containing the
Tessaly part number, the hardware revision, and the date of manufacture in the format
defined in TSA-LB-077. The label shall remain legible for the service life of the vehicle.
The label position is shown on drawing TSA-DRW-1188, sheet 2.

TSA 4.2 Traceability records shall be retained for at least 10 years.

TSA 5 Problem Solving
TSA 5.1 The supplier shall provide an initial containment response within 24 hours of
notification of a nonconformance and shall complete the root cause analysis using the 8D
format defined in TSA-QP-050.

TSA 5.2 Initial containment responses shall be submitted no earlier than 72 hours after
notification of a nonconformance, once sorting results are available.

TSA 5.3 Warranty part analysis shall be performed according to TSA-QP-050 and TSA-WR-090.
