TESSALY AUTOMOTIVE
Material Specification for Battery Module Components
Document ID: TSA-MAT-420
Revision: C
Effective Date: 2025-12-01
All names, companies and document identifiers in this document are fictional.

1 Scope
1.1 This specification applies to materials used in battery module assemblies.

2 Adhesives
2.1 Cell housing adhesives shall be cured at a minimum temperature of 80 °C for 20 minutes.

2.2 Adhesive lot certificates shall be retained for at least 5 years.

3 Coatings
3.1 Cell coatings shall meet the dielectric strength requirements of Specification
TSA-CT-077.

3.2 Coating thickness shall be measured according to TSA-CT-080 at a frequency of one sample
per shift.
