TESSALY AUTOMOTIVE
Customer-Specific Requirements for Battery Module Suppliers
Document ID: TSA-CSR-400
Revision: B
Effective Date: 2026-03-20
All names, companies and document identifiers in this document are fictional.

1 General
1.1 This document applies to suppliers of high-voltage battery modules for Tessaly
Automotive.

1.2 Production part approval shall be obtained according to the Production Part Approval
Procedure TSA-PPAP-410 before the first production shipment.

1.3 Materials used in battery modules shall conform to TSA-MAT-420.

1.4 Where this document conflicts with TSA-PPAP-410, the requirements of this document shall
take precedence.

2 Materials
2.1 Specification TSA-CT-077 does not apply to battery modules and shall not be used for
cell coatings.

2.2 Cell housing adhesives shall have a maximum curing temperature of 60 °C.

2.3 Thermal interface materials shall be qualified according to TSA-MAT-425, and the
qualification report shall be included in the production part approval package described in
TSA-PPAP-410.

3 Approval
3.1 The supplier shall submit the production part approval package at least 20 working days
before the planned start of production.

3.2 The approval package shall include the dimensional results report described in the
Production Part Approval Procedure.

3.3 Run-at-rate results shall be documented on form TSA-QF-031A.

3.4 The supplier shall perform the run-at-rate verification during the first week of series
production.
